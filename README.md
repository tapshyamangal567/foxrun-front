## 7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

* **Section Headline:** Frequently Asked Questions

1. **How do I book a car?**
   * *Answer:* Simply download our app from the App Store or Google Play, sign up, enter your destination, choose your car type, and confirm your booking. It’s that easy!
2. **Can I schedule a ride in advance?**
   * *Answer:* Yes! Our app allows you to book a ride for an immediate pick-up or schedule one for a future date and time, giving you complete control over your travel plans.
3. **What types of cars are available?**
   * *Answer:* We offer a wide range of options to fit your needs, from budget-friendly economy cars to spacious SUVs and premium luxury vehicles for special occasions.
4. **How is the fare calculated?**
   * *Answer:* Fares are calculated based on distance, time, base fare, and the type of car selected. You will always see an estimated fare before you confirm your booking.
5. **Is there 24/7 customer support?**
   * *Answer:* Absolutely. Our customer support team is available around the clock to assist you with any questions or issues you may have during your ride.
6. **What if I need to cancel my booking?**
   * *Answer:* You can easily cancel your booking through the app. Please refer to our cancellation policy within the app for details on any potential fees.
