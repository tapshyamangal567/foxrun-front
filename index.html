Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a car?

Simply download our app from the App Store or Google Play, sign up, enter your destination, choose your car type, and confirm your booking. It’s that easy!

Can I schedule a ride in advance?

Yes! Our app allows you to book a ride for an immediate pick-up or schedule one for a future date and time, giving you complete control over your travel plans.

What types of cars are available?

We offer a wide range of options to fit your needs, from budget-friendly economy cars to spacious SUVs and premium luxury vehicles for special occasions.

How is the fare calculated?

Fares are calculated based on distance, time, base fare, and the type of car selected. You will always see an estimated fare before you confirm your booking.

Is there 24/7 customer support?

Absolutely. Our customer support team is available around the clock to assist you with any questions or issues you may have during your ride.

What if I need to cancel my booking?

You can easily cancel your booking through the app. Please refer to our cancellation policy within the app for details on any potential fees.
